# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder is the single source of truth for how OctoAcme runs projects. Use it to onboard new teammates and to keep project management practices consistent across teams.

## Summary of OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme follows a lightweight, iterative project management lifecycle:

1. **Initiation** - validate the problem, align stakeholders and get the work authorized with a short project charter.
2. **Planning** - break the approved initiative into milestones, epics and a prioritized backlog with estimates and owners.
3. **Execution & tracking** - deliver in short iterations with stand-ups, boards and regular status updates against milestones.
4. **Risks & communication** - keep a risk register, manage dependencies and communicate status and escalations on a predictable cadence.
5. **Release & deployment** - ship through a repeatable release checklist with testing, approvals and rollback plans.
6. **Retrospective & continuous improvement** - reflect after each iteration or release and turn lessons learned into action items.

Clear roles and personas (developers, project managers, product owners, stakeholders and more) make ownership explicit at every stage.
